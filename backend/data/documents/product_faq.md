# Product FAQ - TechNova SmartHub

## General

### What is TechNova SmartHub?
TechNova SmartHub is an AI-powered home automation controller that connects and manages all your smart home devices from a single interface. It supports WiFi, Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Bluetooth protocols.

### What devices are compatible?
SmartHub is compatible with over 5,000 devices including smart lights, thermostats, door locks, cameras, speakers, and appliances from major brands like Philips Hue, Nest, Ring, Sonos, and Samsung SmartThings.

### What are the available models?
- **SmartHub Lite ($99):** Supports up to 30 devices, WiFi and Bluetooth only.
- **SmartHub Pro ($199):** Supports up to 100 devices, all protocols, includes voice assistant.
- **SmartHub Enterprise ($499):** Unlimited devices, all protocols, advanced automation, dedicated support.

## Setup & Installation

### How do I set up my SmartHub?
1. Download the TechNova app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Plug in your SmartHub and wait for the LED to flash blue.
3. Open the app and follow the on-screen pairing instructions.
4. Setup typically takes 5-10 minutes.

### Does SmartHub require internet?
SmartHub requires internet for initial setup and cloud features (remote access, voice assistant). Basic local automation works without internet after initial setup.

## Technical Specifications

- **Processor:** Quad-core ARM Cortex-A72, 1.8 GHz
- **Memory:** 4GB RAM, 32GB storage
- **Connectivity:** WiFi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, Zigbee 3.0, Z-Wave Plus
- **Power:** 12V DC, 2A adapter included
- **Dimensions:** 12cm x 12cm x 3cm
- **Weight:** 280g

## Warranty

All SmartHub products come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, water exposure, or unauthorized modifications. Extended warranty (additional 1 year) is available for $29.

## Subscription Plans

- **Basic (Free):** Local automation, up to 5 routines, community support.
- **Plus ($4.99/month):** Cloud backup, unlimited routines, remote access, email support.
- **Premium ($9.99/month):** Everything in Plus + priority support, advanced analytics, early access to features.
