# Returns & Refund Policy - TechNova Inc.

## Return Window

Customers may return any product within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund, provided the item is in its original condition with all packaging and accessories.

## Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a return:
- The product must be unused or show only minor signs of testing.
- Original packaging, manuals, and all included accessories must be present.
- Proof of purchase (order confirmation email or receipt) is required.
- Products with physical damage caused by the customer are not eligible for return.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
- Gift cards and promotional vouchers
- Software licenses once activated
- Items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Personalized or custom-configured products

## Refund Process

1. Initiate a return request via the TechNova website or by contacting support@technova.com.
2. You will receive a prepaid return shipping label within 2 business days.
3. Ship the product within 7 days of receiving the label.
4. Refund is processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

### Refund Methods
- **Original payment method:** Full refund to the card/account used for purchase.
- **Store credit:** Available immediately upon return approval (10% bonus credit added).

## Exchanges

Products may be exchanged for a different model or variant within the 30-day window. If the new item costs more, the difference will be charged. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

## Defective Products

Products with manufacturing defects can be returned within the 2-year warranty period at no cost. TechNova will either repair, replace, or refund at its discretion.

## Late Returns

Returns initiated after the 30-day window but within 60 days may be accepted at TechNova's discretion with a 15% restocking fee. Returns after 60 days are not accepted.
