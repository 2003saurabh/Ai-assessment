# HR Leave Policy - TechNova Inc.

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month from the date of joining.

### Carry Forward
- Unused leave up to a maximum of 5 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
- Carried-forward leave must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or it will lapse.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### Extended Illness
For illnesses extending beyond 12 days, employees may apply for unpaid medical leave of up to 30 additional days with HR approval and a medical certificate from a registered practitioner.

## Parental Leave

- **Maternity Leave:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave for birth mothers.
- **Paternity Leave:** 4 weeks of fully paid leave for fathers/partners, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
- **Adoption Leave:** 12 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver.

## Public Holidays

TechNova observes 10 public holidays per year as determined by the regional office calendar published in January.

## Leave Application Process

1. Submit leave requests via the HR Portal at least 5 business days in advance for planned leave.
2. Emergency/sick leave must be reported to your direct manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence.
3. All leave requests require manager approval.
4. Leave during project-critical periods may be denied or rescheduled at management discretion.

## Probation Period

Employees on probation (first 6 months) are entitled to 1 day of leave per month. Sick leave accrues at the standard rate but annual leave entitlement begins after probation completion.
